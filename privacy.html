One Lever — Privacy Policy

One Lever does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no backend server, and no network connection of its own — every calculation (your quiz answers and your results) happens entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Your answers and results are not saved after you close the app. The only thing the app keeps on your device is a single yes/no flag so that it asks you for an App Store review at most once.

We use no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs. Because we never receive any data from you, there is nothing to disclose to third parties and no server-side record of you to access, correct, or delete.

If you choose to share your result, iOS's share sheet handles it; what you send, and to whom, is entirely under your control. If you email us directly, we'll only use what you send to respond to you.
