One Lever — Privacy Policy (TestFlight beta)

One Lever does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app has no account system, no backend server, and no network connection of its own — every calculation happens entirely on your device.

The TestFlight beta build can, only if you turn it on, compose a plain-text feedback message from your quiz answers (as option codes), your result numbers, your answer to "Could you do this?", whether you tapped Share, how long the quiz took, how many times you've started and finished it, the last question you reached in a quiz you didn't finish, and, if you tap "Refine this estimate", which part you said you'd refine. Nothing is sent automatically: the message exists only when you tap "Send beta feedback" and choose where to send it in the iOS share sheet. It contains no device identifier, no name, and no location beyond the state you selected in the quiz. Those counters and that question number live in the app's own storage on your device and are deleted with the app.

Apart from that opt-in message, the beta build behaves like the release build: no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting beyond what TestFlight itself provides under Apple's terms, and no third-party SDKs.
